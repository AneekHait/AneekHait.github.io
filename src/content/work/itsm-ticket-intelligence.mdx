## Context

In high-volume enterprise IT environments (ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, BMC Remedy), queue reporting often relies on superficial vanity metrics: raw ticket counts and generic category breakdowns.

These summary reports obscure the structural inefficiencies that actually inflate support costs:
1. **The "Hot Potato" Problem**: Incidents bounced between multiple support tiers before reaching an engineer capable of resolving them.
2. **Distorted Quality Metrics**: Summing customer satisfaction ratings rather than tracking genuine averages and distribution percentiles.
3. **Unrealized Deflection Potential**: Failure to isolate high-frequency, low-complexity tickets that can be shifted left to self-service or automated resolution.

To give IT service delivery leaders actionable visibility over these operational dynamics, I built the **ITSM Ticket Intelligence Command Center** on Tableau Public.

## Analytical Core & Architecture

The dashboard models **20,000 realistic enterprise ITIL incident records** across 4 core analytical modules:

### 1. Reassignment Hop Bottleneck Matrix
Tickets rarely fail because of single-agent inefficiency; they fail because of hand-off friction. The matrix crosses technical domains (Cloud, IAM, Network, Database, Hardware, Software) against escalation depth ($0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5+$ hops). 

The data reveals a stark operational threshold: tickets that hop $3+$ times experience an exponential 400% increase in resolution duration and a corresponding 35% decline in user satisfaction. This pinpoints exact triage gaps where front-line routing rules require automation.

### 2. CSAT Statistical Integrity
Many operational dashboards make the novice error of reporting `SUM(CSAT Score)`, producing arbitrary figures in the tens of thousands. This dashboard restores rigorous statistical standards by tracking `AVG(CSAT Score)` on the authentic 1.0–5.0 scale across support tiers (Tier 1: `4.22`, Tier 2: `4.21`, Tier 3: `4.19`), complemented by exact mark labels.

### 3. Shift-Left Deflection & FinOps ROI
Rather than treating all resolved tickets as equal, the model categorizes incidents into actionable operational dispositions:
- **Automate via Self-Service / Bot**: High-volume, low-friction tasks (e.g. password resets, MFA re-enrollment).
- **Eradicate via Engineering Root Cause**: Recurring platform bugs (e.g. database connection pool exhaustion).
- **Standardize via KBA / SOP**: Repeatable workflows suitable for front-line delegation.
- **Retain Human Tier 3 Support**: Genuine architectural edge cases.

By applying tier-weighted hourly cost coefficients, the dashboard quantifies **$2.4M in addressable annual support cost reductions**, translating technical queue hygiene into concrete business value for executive stakeholders.

## Key Insights

1. **First-Contact Resolution is the Strongest CSAT Driver**: Zero-hop resolutions average a 4.65 CSAT, dropping to 3.10 once an incident traverses more than three assignment groups.
2. **Triage Automation Yields Higher ROI Than Headcount**: Eliminating routing hops saves more cumulative engineering hours than adding front-line service desk staff.
3. **Shift-Left Priority**: 38% of Tier 1 and Tier 2 volume represents candidates for self-service deflection or automated runbooks.
