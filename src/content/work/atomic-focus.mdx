## Context

Deep work is easily derailed by two friction points: overwhelming tasks with ambiguous starting points, and ambient distractions. Traditional timers count minutes, but do nothing to reduce cognitive load.

## Product Overview

**Atomic Focus** is a free, distraction-free AI Pomodoro timer built for developers, analysts, and students to enter flow state faster:

- **AI Task Decomposition**: Breaks complex, overwhelming objectives into actionable 15–25 minute "atomic" micro-steps with time estimates.
- **Multi-Track Sound Mixer**: Blends customizable ambient soundscapes (Binaural Beats, Lo-Fi, Rain, Coffee Shop, Forest, White Noise) powered by the Web Audio API.
- **Custom Flow Intervals**: Classic 25/5 Pomodoro, 50/10 Ultradian rhythm, or fully customized work/break cycles with visual progress rings.
- **Privacy & Speed**: Zero friction, no mandatory account setup, fast client-side performance, and dark-first aesthetic.
