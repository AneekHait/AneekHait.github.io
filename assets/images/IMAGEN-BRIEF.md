# Imagen Data-Studio Brief

The portfolio hero is intentionally not portrait-led. Its primary visual is the live CSS signal map in the page. Imagen may supply only a subtle non-photographic texture behind that visualization after review.

## Supporting Texture

- Suggested output: `hero-data-studio.webp`
- Master size: 2400 x 1500 before WebP optimization
- Prompt:

> Create a tactile editorial still life about business intelligence and analytical reasoning, with no people and no readable interface. Use physical paper strips, translucent acrylic planes, precise measurement marks, restrained chart-like vertical forms, and a dark neutral studio surface. Compose the forms on the right half and preserve calm low-detail negative space on the left. Use coral, teal, and chartreuse only as small functional accents. Sophisticated data-publication art direction, crisp geometry, soft directional light, realistic material texture, generous black and charcoal areas. No portrait, face, person, hands, text, letters, numbers, logos, fake dashboards, screens, floating holograms, neon glow, gradients, decorative orbs, or science-fiction UI.

## Acceptance Check

Reject an output if it includes a person, readable text, invented dashboard controls, fake metrics, visual clutter, or synthetic geometry. The image must remain secondary to the real HTML name, positioning copy, signal map, and authentic project screenshots.

Optimize an approved master to WebP at roughly 350 KB or less. Apply it only as a low-contrast background layer within `.hero-visual`; retain the CSS grid, signal marks, legend, and mobile fallback.
