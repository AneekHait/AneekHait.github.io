Project 02

Titanic Survival Analysis

A familiar dataset pushed beyond familiar conclusions.

  • 1,309passengers studied
  • 11.3xsurvival odds
  • 49passing tests
Question
Which factors truly drove survival, and which apparent relationships were confounded?
Method
Wilson confidence intervals, odds ratios, effect sizes, hypothesis tests, and class-by-sex stratification across 1,309 passengers.
Finding
Sex was the dominant predictor: women had 11.3 times the survival odds of men, with class sharply compounding the effect.
